Integer Operations Cheat Sheet

+ Adding Integers

  • Same Signs: Add absolute values and keep the sign.
    (+5) + (+3) = +8 | (-5) + (-3) = -8
  • Different Signs: Subtract smaller from larger, take sign of larger absolute value.
    (-8) + (+3) = -5 | (+9) + (-4) = +5

− Subtracting Integers ("KCC" Rule)

Keep, Change, Change: Keep 1st number, Change subtraction to addition, Change sign of 2nd number.

Example: (+7) − (-4) → (+7) + (+4) = 11

× Multiplication & Division

Same Signs = POSITIVE
(+) × (+) = (+)
(−) × (−) = (+)
Different Signs = NEGATIVE
(+) × (−) = (−)
(−) × (+) = (−)
