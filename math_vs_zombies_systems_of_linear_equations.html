Topic Concept Overview: Systems of Linear Equations

Understand the fundamentals before proceeding to the step-by-step walkthroughs.

1. What is a System?

A System of Linear Equations consists of two or more linear equations involving the same variables (such as $x$ and $y$).

$\begin{cases} Ax + By = C \\ Dx + Ey = F \end{cases}$
2. What Does "Solving" Mean?

Solving a system means finding an ordered pair $(x, y)$ that satisfies BOTH equations at the exact same time (their point of intersection).

Point of Intersection: $(x, y)$
3. Which Method to Choose?

Substitution: Best when a variable is isolated or has a coefficient of $1$ or $-1$.
Elimination: Best when coefficients align easily for addition or subtraction.

Sunflower Math Vault & Formula Cheatsheet

Quick reference rules, system classifications, and key algebraic formulas.

Grade 8 Core Reference

Standard Form

📐
$Ax + By = C$

Where $A$, $B$, and $C$ are integers (preferably $A \ge 0$). Variables $x$ and $y$ are aligned on the left side of the equation.

Substitution Rule

🌱
$y = \text{expression in } x$

Isolate one variable in one equation, then substitute that expression into the second equation to solve for a single variable.

Elimination Rule

⚔️
$+Ay + (-Ay) = 0$

Multiply equations by constants so that coefficients of one variable are opposites, then add equations together to eliminate that variable.

1 Solution (Intersecting)

🎯
$\frac{a_1}{a_2} \neq \frac{b_1}{b_2}$

The lines intersect at exactly one point $(x, y)$. The system is Consistent and Independent.

No Solution (Parallel)

🚫
$\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{b_1}{b_2} \neq \frac{c_1}{c_2}$

The lines are parallel and never meet. The system is Inconsistent.

Infinite Solutions

♾️
$\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{b_1}{b_2} = \frac{c_1}{c_2}$

The lines overlap completely (same line). The system is Consistent and Dependent.
